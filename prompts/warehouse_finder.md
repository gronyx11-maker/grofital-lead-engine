# Grofital Warehouse & 3PL Lead Finder (second lead stream)

Follow prompts/lead_finder.md for all rules (public sources only, verification, scoring, compliance by region, hard rules, never send anything). This file adds what is specific to warehouse and logistics businesses.

## Target
- Small and mid-size warehousing, 3PL, contract logistics, fulfilment, cold-chain, and freight-forwarding businesses with their own warehouse space.
- Roughly 10-250 employees, 1-10 sites. Owner-operated, family-run, or PE/lender-backed. Exclude global 3PLs and public giants.
- Regions: United States, Europe, UAE, Australia. Dedupe on website domain against leads/warehouse_leads.csv and leads/leads.csv.

## Buying triggers (need one, dated source)
New or expanded facility, new anchor contract, acquisition or ownership change, funding or lender facility, WMS/ERP or automation rollout, finance-role job posts (controller, finance manager, FP&A, data/BI analyst), new CFO or leadership change, entry into e-commerce fulfilment or cold chain.

## What to record (leads/warehouse_leads.csv, one row per business)
Full business profile: HQ, sites and capacity (sq ft / pallet positions if public), services, verticals and named public customers, systems (WMS/ERP if public), ownership, year founded, employees, trigger and date, source URLs, public contact route, decision-maker if public, confidence, and fields left "Unverified".
Plus the pitch plan: likely pain points, pitch packages, pitch angle, opening line, best channel and timing, likely objections.
Then write outreach/warehouse/YYYY-MM-DD_company.md: business profile, why now, what to pitch, how to pitch, email (<120 words), LinkedIn note (<300 chars), follow-ups at 3 and 7 days.

## What to pitch (pick 1-2 packages per lead, tied to the trigger)
Data analysis:
- A1 Warehouse KPI dashboard: OTIF, pick accuracy, dock-to-stock, order cycle time, labour productivity, space utilisation, from WMS/ERP exports.
- A2 Cost-to-serve and customer profitability: margin per client, SKU and service line, to find loss-making contracts.
- A3 Billing leakage review: unbilled storage, handling and accessorial charges, rate-card mismatches.
- A4 Volume and capacity forecasting: inbound/outbound peaks, labour and space planning, inventory ageing.
Fractional CFO:
- C1 3PL pricing and contract model: cost-plus / open-book pricing, tender and renewal pricing, margin guardrails.
- C2 Cash flow and working capital: receivables on 45-90 day terms, fuel/labour/lease scenarios, 13-week cash forecast, lender reporting.
- C3 Expansion and capex business case: new site, lease vs build, racking and automation ROI, financing pack.
- C4 Deal readiness: monthly management accounts, KPI pack for lenders/buyers, M&A due-diligence support.

## How to pitch
- Lead with the trigger and one hard fact (new site, contract, hire). Talk in operator terms (margin per pallet position, utilisation, billing accuracy), not finance jargon.
- Offer a small first step: a one-off billing-leakage check, a KPI dashboard on one site, or a 13-week cash forecast. No long retainers up front.
- Typical decision-makers: owner/MD, COO or GM, finance manager. Owner-operators often have no finance lead; mid-size firms often have a bookkeeper but no FP&A.
- Likely objections: "we already have an accountant" (we do forward-looking decisions, not compliance), "WMS already reports" (we join WMS to financials), cost (fixed-fee pilot). Never invent client results.
- Region tone and compliance: follow STEP 7 of prompts/lead_finder.md.
